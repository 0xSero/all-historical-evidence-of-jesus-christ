# Dilexit nos — encyclical on the human and divine love of the Heart of Jesus (2024)

> Pope Francis, 24 October 2024. Full text: <https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/encyclicals/documents/20241024-enciclica-dilexit-nos.html>

An encyclical on devotion to the Sacred Heart. It reads that devotion through scripture,
the church fathers, and later mystical writers, and it treats the heart as a symbol of the
unity of a person's affections and will.

Section 119 is the passage the dossier cites: it recalls the reported visions of Margaret
Mary Alacoque in 1673-1675, which are the origin of the modern First Friday devotion and
of the specific form of the devotion.

The dossier cites the encyclical as an example of present-day reception: a 2024 papal
document grounded in a seventeenth-century visionary experience. It is evidence of what a
church teaches now, not of what happened in the first century.
