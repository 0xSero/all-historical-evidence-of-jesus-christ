# Norms for Proceeding in the Discernment of Alleged Supernatural Phenomena (2024)

> Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, 17 May 2024. Full text: <https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20240517_norme-fenomeni-soprannaturali_en.html>

Procedural norms, approved by Pope Francis, for how the Dicastery and local bishops
evaluate reports of apparitions, visions, and similar claims. The document replaces older
practice with a graded set of conclusions — including that a phenomenon may be judged
worthy of belief, or that it shows signs of a non-supernatural origin — rather than a
single binary verdict.

The dossier cites it for the framework a major church body now uses when assessing reports
of revelation, which is directly relevant to how modern claims about Jesus are evaluated
institutionally.

**The full text is not mirrored in this archive.** It is published by the Holy See at the
link above under its copyright.

What it establishes: the current institutional procedure. It does not adjudicate any
particular claim, and it is not evidence for or against any reported event.
