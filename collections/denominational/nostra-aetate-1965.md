# Nostra aetate — Declaration on the Relation of the Church to Non-Christian Religions (1965)

> Second Vatican Council, promulgated 28 October 1965. Full text: <https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651028_nostra-aetate_en.html>

A short declaration of the Second Vatican Council in five sections. Section 4 concerns the
Jewish people. It states that what happened in the passion of Christ cannot be charged to
all Jews then living, nor to Jews today, and it condemns "hatred, persecutions, displays
of antisemitism" directed against Jews.

The dossier cites it for one point: the Catholic Church's own repudiation of collective
guilt for the crucifixion, as a modern reception of the passion narratives rather than as
historical evidence about who was responsible.

**The full text is not mirrored in this archive.** The Holy See publishes it online at the
link above and asserts copyright over its documents. Read it at the source.

What a quotation from this document can establish: the position of one church at one date.
It is a modern doctrinal statement, not evidence about first-century events.
