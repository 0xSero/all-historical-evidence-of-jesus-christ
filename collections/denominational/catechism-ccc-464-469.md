# Catechism of the Catholic Church, paragraphs 464-469 ("True God and True Man")

> Catechism of the Catholic Church, Article 3 of the section on the Incarnation. Full text: <https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P1J.HTM>

This subsection of the Catechism sets out the Catholic doctrine of the incarnation: that
Jesus Christ is one person with two natures, divine and human; that he is "true God and
true man"; and that the two natures are united without confusion, change, division, or
separation.

It is a confessional formulation built on the councils of Nicaea (325), Chalcedon (451),
and later teaching. The dossier cites it as the clearest short statement of what orthodox
Christian doctrine claims about Jesus, in contrast to the historical questions the dossier
tracks.

**The full text is not mirrored in this archive.** The Catechism is published by the Holy
See at the link above and carries a Librarian of the Holy Roman Church copyright notice.

The distinction the dossier keeps applies here: a catechism states what the church
teaches. That is a claim to be reported accurately, not evidence that the teaching is
true, and it does not bear on the historical reconstruction of Jesus's life.
