# Common Christological Declaration between the Catholic Church and the Assyrian Church of the East (1994)

> Pope John Paul II and Mar Dinkha IV, 11 November 1994. Full text: <https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/pontifical_councils/chrstuni/documents/rc_pc_chrstuni_doc_11111994_assyrian-church_en.html>

A joint declaration signed in Rome by Pope John Paul II and Mar Dinkha IV, Catholicos-
Patriarch of the Assyrian Church of the East. It resolves a christological dispute dating
from the Council of Ephesus (431): the two churches affirm a common faith that the one
person of Christ is both divine and human, while acknowledging that their traditions use
different formulas — "one nature" language in the Assyrian tradition, "two natures" in the
Catholic — for the same confession.

The dossier cites it as a case of a doctrinal division over how to describe Jesus being
resolved by agreement on substance rather than by one side adopting the other's wording.

**The full text is not mirrored in this archive.** It is published by the Holy See at the
link above; the document carries a Libreria Editrice Vaticana copyright notice.

Its evidence value is about the churches' self-understanding in 1994, not about the
first-century Jesus.
