## Sources and further reading

This register contains 238 reference entries. Some entries point to the same source
because different catalogue sections use it for different questions. Biblical
chapter-and-verse citations in the text are additional primary references. Linked scholarly
articles, editions, museum records, institutional reports, and religious communities' own
statements serve different evidentiary purposes.

Access date for the retrieved resources: 26 October 2026. Where a source is a publisher description, catalogue
record, indexed abstract, or fragment preserved by another author, its limits are identified. A citation does not
imply that an inaccessible complete book or every manuscript was inspected.
